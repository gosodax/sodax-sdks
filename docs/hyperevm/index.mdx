<Note>
  **No new contracts.** SODAX's HyperEVM spoke contracts are already live; your entire integration is TypeScript. Your users sign ordinary EVM transactions on HyperEVM, and SODAX coordinates cross-network execution, routing, and recovery behind the scenes.
</Note>

You keep shipping on HyperEVM. SODAX absorbs the cross-network execution layer, so your product can offer trading and lending against every asset in the SODAX System, <span data-sodax-config="count" data-metric="swap-tokens">166</span> across all supported networks, without leaving your stack. Integration happens in TypeScript, at the SDK or React layer, against HyperEVM. There is nothing to deploy, audit, or maintain on-chain.

Using SODAX is free. Trades carry a fixed 0.1% base fee taken by the protocol, and you set your own platform fee on top. That part is fully yours, both the rate and the revenue. See [Monetize SDK](/developers/how-to/monetize_sdk).

## Two things that differ from other EVM networks

To the SDK, HyperEVM is an EVM network like any other: chain ID 999, HYPE as the native gas token, the same wallet provider and approvals. What sets it apart is its place inside Hyperliquid:

- **HyperCore balances are not HyperEVM balances.** Hyperliquid has two parts, HyperCore (spot, perps, and the order books) and HyperEVM. SODAX acts on HyperEVM only. Funds a user holds in HyperCore spot or perps are invisible to the SDK and cannot fund an intent until the user transfers them to HyperEVM (the "Transfer to/from EVM" action in Hyperliquid's app).
- **Swaps can deliver USDC straight into HyperCore.** The SDK ships a delivery hook for HyperEVM: set `hook: { kind: HookKind.HYPERCORE_DEPOSIT }` on a swap whose output is USDC on HyperEVM, and the output is deposited into the recipient's HyperCore perps account instead of their HyperEVM wallet. See [Delivering into HyperCore](/hyperevm/swaps#delivering-into-hypercore).

## Scope and build with AI

Point your AI coding assistant at the [SODAX Builders MCP](/builders-mcp) and it gains live access to the whole SODAX System: supported chains and tokens, real swap quotes, money market rates, intent history, and these docs. It works in Claude, Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf, and any other MCP-capable client.

```json
{
  "mcpServers": {
    "sodax-builders": {
      "url": "https://builders.sodax.com/mcp"
    }
  }
}
```

Use it to scope before you commit: your assistant can read your own codebase, pull real quotes and token lists, and map exactly where SODAX fits in your product. Full tool list and per-client setup at [builders.sodax.com](https://builders.sodax.com/).
