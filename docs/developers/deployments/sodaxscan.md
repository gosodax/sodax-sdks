Available at [sodaxscan.com](https://sodaxscan.com). Look up source/destination tx hashes and delivery status for cross-network messages.

For solver fill status on a swap intent (including `fill_tx_hash` when solved), poll `sodax.swaps.getStatus` — see [Make a swap (SDK)](/developers/how-to/how_to_make_a_swap).
