## Who this page is for

This page is for wallets, exchanges and partners that review Robinhood stock tokens before offering them through SODAX. The answers apply on every network the stocks are on. The integration steps are in [Add Robinhood stocks to your app](/developers/how-to/robinhood-stocks). Backing per network, the path back to Robinhood Chain, fees and limits are in the [Robinhood stocks FAQ](/developers/how-to/robinhood-stocks-faq).

## Due diligence answers

#### Who issues the tokens, and what is SODAX's relationship to Robinhood?

Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited issues the underlying Stock Tokens on Robinhood Chain. On every other network the stocks are on, SODAX issues the tokens, which wrap Robinhood tokens locked on Robinhood Chain. SODAX's development is supported by the ICON Foundation, a Swiss foundation. SODAX is independent of Robinhood and does not act on its behalf. To confirm that an address is a Robinhood Stock Token on Robinhood Chain, Robinhood's [Token Contracts](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/contracts) page is the canonical list.

Robinhood describes Stock Tokens as "tokenised debt securities" that give economic exposure to the underlying shares and ETFs but no legal or beneficial rights in them. See its [Stock Tokens documentation](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-tokens).

#### Are the tokens backed 1:1?

Yes, on every network. For each stock, the SODAX SpokeAssetManager on Robinhood Chain holds one Robinhood token for every token users hold on all the other networks combined. The Robinhood stocks FAQ explains [how the supply works on each network](/developers/how-to/robinhood-stocks-faq#supply) and [how to check the backing on-chain](/developers/how-to/robinhood-stocks-faq#check-backing).

#### How is the supply on other networks tied to the lock on Robinhood Chain? {#tied-to-lock}

Tokens move between networks only through the SODAX [Asset Manager](/developers/technical-overview/asset-manager), and a spoke releases tokens only on a verified message from the hub. The steps are in [How does a stock reach another network?](/developers/how-to/robinhood-stocks-faq#how-it-moves), and how each network issues its supply is in [How is the supply issued on each network?](/developers/how-to/robinhood-stocks-faq#supply). On Hedera, the token's supply key can also mint new supply to the treasury account outside that flow, so check the treasury balance as well as the SpokeAssetManager's when you [verify the backing](/developers/how-to/robinhood-stocks#verify-backing).

#### Who controls the token contracts? {#token-controls}

- **Robinhood Chain:** the tokens are issued and governed by Robinhood, not SODAX. See Robinhood's [Stock Tokens](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-tokens) page for their controls.
- **Hedera:** the stock tokens use Hedera's native token service (HTS). They have no KYC, freeze, wipe or pause keys. Each token does have an admin key, which can update or delete the token, and a supply key, which can mint new supply. The supply type is infinite. You can read the keys and treasury for each token from the Hedera mirror node (`https://mainnet-public.mirrornode.hedera.com/api/v1/tokens/<token id>`); check them there before you rely on them.
- **Stellar:** each stock is a classic Stellar asset with a Stellar Asset Contract. The issuer account for each ticker is in the Stellar spoke config (`trustlineConfigs` in [`chains.ts`](https://github.com/gosodax/sodax-sdks/blob/main/packages/types/src/chains/chains.ts)). Its authorization flags (`auth_required`, `auth_revocable`, `auth_clawback_enabled`) are off. The issuer account is a multisig and can issue more of the asset. Check the flags and signers before you rely on them, on Horizon (`https://horizon.stellar.org/accounts/<issuer>`) or any Stellar explorer.
- **Sonic:** each ticker is a vault token. The vault has an owner that controls its token configuration and rate limits, and the rate limits apply to mints, burns and transfers ([Vault Token](/developers/technical-overview/vault-token)).

SODAX holds the admin and upgrade keys for its side of the flow on every network: the SpokeAssetManager on each network, the Hedera admin and supply keys, the Stellar issuer account and the Sonic vault owner. Partners integrating SODAX deploy no contracts and hold no admin key for any part of the flow.

#### Are swaps custodial?

Swaps are non-custodial: users sign from their own wallets. The Robinhood tokens backing the wrapped tokens are held in SODAX protocol contracts.

#### Who is responsible for the geo restrictions?

Robinhood's [Stock Tokens documentation](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-tokens) states that Stock Tokens may not be offered, sold, or delivered in the United States or to U.S. persons. It also states that offers and sales are restricted in other jurisdictions. The list is on Robinhood's [restricted jurisdictions page](https://docs.robinhood.com/rhj/restricted-jurisdictions). Review those terms for your product. This page does not restate them.

Partners that offer stock tokens through SODAX are responsible for compliance under sections 6.3 and 6.5 of the [SODAX API Terms](https://www.sodax.com/api-terms), on whichever network they offer them. That means checking users' eligibility, blocking restricted jurisdictions, obtaining users' confirmation of eligibility and showing the issuer's notices. A partner that cannot do this must not offer stock tokens (section 6.5). A quote for a stock does not mean a trade is permitted for that user. SODAX may tag these assets, require partners to opt in, or stop routing them at any time.

#### Are wallet addresses screened against sanctions lists?

Yes. SODAX screens wallet addresses against sanctions lists before swaps and bridges execute. Section 6.2 of the [SODAX API Terms](https://www.sodax.com/api-terms) covers swaps.

#### Where are the audit reports?

The published reports are on the [Audits](/developers/audits) page, with the PDFs in the [sodax-sdks Audits folder](https://github.com/gosodax/sodax-sdks/tree/main/Audits). Robinhood Chain and Hedera run the same audited EVM spoke contracts as the other EVM networks SODAX supports, and adding them needed no code changes on SODAX's side. So there is no separate audit for these two networks: the [Collaborative (Aug 2025)](https://github.com/gosodax/sodax-sdks/blob/main/Audits/2025.08.01%20-%20Final%20-%20Sodax%20Collaborative%20Audit%20Report%201754078902%20(1)%20(1).pdf) report covers that code. Its scope lists the SpokeAssetManager and its withdrawal rate limit, and the hub Asset Manager and asset token. The Stellar contracts are covered by the Soroban report on that page.

#### Who provides the liquidity, and how deep is it?

On every network, liquidity comes from the existing Robinhood Chain market for each stock, where every stock trades against USDG (see [Gotchas](/developers/how-to/robinhood-stocks#gotchas)). That market is the Uniswap pools on Robinhood Chain, and solvers use them to fill each swap. For a ticker's depth and the price impact of a given order size, look up its pools on [Uniswap](https://app.uniswap.org/explore/pools) with Robinhood Chain selected. Minimum sizes apply to stock-to-stock swaps. Network-to-network bridges have their own limit. See [`getBridgeableAmount`](/developers/packages/foundation/sdk/functional-modules/bridge#getbridgeableamount). How each ticker is priced is in [Live tickers and networks](/developers/how-to/robinhood-stocks#live-tickers).
