## Who this page is for

Wallets, exchanges and partners who review Robinhood stock tokens before offering them through SODAX. The integration steps are in [Add Robinhood stocks to your app](/developers/how-to/robinhood-stocks); this page answers the due diligence questions that come before it.

## Questions

#### Who issues the tokens, and what is SODAX's relationship to Robinhood?

The underlying Stock Tokens are issued by Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited. The tokens on Hedera and Stellar are made available through the SODAX protocol, which wraps Robinhood tokens locked on Robinhood Chain. SODAX is independent of Robinhood and does not act on its behalf.

Robinhood describes Stock Tokens as "tokenised debt securities" that give economic exposure to the underlying shares and ETFs but no legal or beneficial rights in them; see its [Stock Tokens documentation](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-tokens).

The locked Robinhood tokens on Robinhood Chain back the Hedera and Stellar tokens together. The published script in [Verify backing on-chain](/developers/how-to/robinhood-stocks#verify-backing) covers Hedera only, so a passing check does not prove full backing on its own, and this page does not state that the Stellar backing is verified. A holder can bridge back to the same stock on Robinhood Chain through the [bridge path](/developers/how-to/robinhood-stocks#bridge-path), up to the bridge limit at that moment. How the backing works is in [What it is](/developers/how-to/robinhood-stocks#what-it-is).

#### Are swaps custodial?

Swaps are non-custodial: users sign from their own wallets. The Robinhood tokens backing the wrapped tokens are held in SODAX protocol contracts.

#### Who is responsible for the geo restrictions?

Robinhood's [Stock Tokens documentation](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-tokens) states that Stock Tokens may not be offered, sold, or delivered in the United States or to U.S. persons, and that offers and sales are restricted in other jurisdictions; the list is on Robinhood's [restricted jurisdictions page](https://docs.robinhood.com/rhj/restricted-jurisdictions). Review those terms for your product; this page does not restate them. Wallet addresses are screened against sanctions lists before swaps and bridges execute (section 6.2 of the [SODAX API Terms](https://www.sodax.com/api-terms)). This page covers swaps and bridges; the screening scope for Stock Tokens used as money-market collateral is not documented here.

Partners that offer stock tokens through SODAX are responsible for compliance under sections 6.3 and 6.5 of the API Terms: checking users' eligibility, blocking restricted jurisdictions, obtaining users' confirmation of eligibility and showing the issuer's notices. A partner that cannot do this must not offer stock tokens (section 6.5). A quote for a stock does not mean a trade is permitted for that user, and SODAX may tag these assets, require partners to opt in, or stop routing them at any time.

#### Where are the audit reports?

The published reports are on the [Audits](/developers/audits) page, with the PDFs in the [sodax-sdks Audits folder](https://github.com/gosodax/sodax-sdks/tree/main/Audits). The audits index does not yet say which reports cover the Hedera and Robinhood Chain spokes or the Sonic hub vaults, so this page makes no audit-coverage claim for them.

#### Who provides the liquidity, and how deep is it?

Liquidity comes from the existing Robinhood Chain market for each stock, where every stock trades against USDG, and solvers fill SODAX swaps from it. SODAX does not run separate liquidity pools on Hedera or Stellar. Swap depth is bounded by those Robinhood Chain pools and by solver fills (see [Gotchas](/developers/how-to/robinhood-stocks#gotchas)). Minimum sizes apply to stock-to-stock swaps. Network-to-network bridges are a separate limit: what can move is bounded by the remaining deposit capacity, and `sodax.bridge.getBridgeableAmount` returns how much can move right now. How each ticker is priced is in [Live tickers and networks](/developers/how-to/robinhood-stocks#live-tickers).
