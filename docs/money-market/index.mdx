Supply collateral from any spoke network and borrow against it. The money market itself lives on the Sonic hub.

Start with the [supply example](/developers/packages/foundation/sdk/functional-modules/money_market#supply-tokens), including its allowance check, then follow [borrowing](/developers/packages/foundation/sdk/functional-modules/money_market#borrow-tokens) if your app needs it.
For failures, use the [money-market error reference](/developers/packages/foundation/sdk/functional-modules/money_market#error-handling).
