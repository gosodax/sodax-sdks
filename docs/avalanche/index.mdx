<Note>
  **No new contracts.** SODAX's Avalanche spoke contracts are already live; your entire integration is TypeScript. Your users sign ordinary EVM transactions on the C-Chain, and SODAX coordinates cross-network execution, routing, and recovery behind the scenes.
</Note>

You keep shipping on Avalanche. SODAX absorbs the cross-network execution layer, so your product can offer trading and lending against every asset in the SODAX System, <span data-sodax-config="count" data-metric="swap-tokens">166</span> across all supported networks, without leaving your stack. Integration happens in TypeScript, at the SDK or React layer, against the C-Chain. There is nothing to deploy, audit, or maintain on-chain.

Using SODAX is free. Trades carry a fixed 0.1% base fee taken by the protocol, and you set your own platform fee on top. That part is fully yours, both the rate and the revenue. See [Monetize SDK](/developers/how-to/monetize_sdk).

## What to know on Avalanche

To the SDK, Avalanche is a standard EVM network. SODAX has no Avalanche-specific code path: the same wallet provider, approvals, and intent flow apply as on any other EVM network. Two scoping notes:

- **C-Chain only.** SODAX runs on the C-Chain (chain ID 43114), the EVM chain of Avalanche's Primary Network. AVAX held on the X-Chain or P-Chain is outside SODAX's reach until the user moves it to the C-Chain.
- **AVAX is the native token.** It pays gas, uses 18 decimals, and needs no approval. Every other Avalanche token in the SODAX list is an ERC-20 and needs an allowance before it is swapped or supplied.

## Scope and build with AI

Point your AI coding assistant at the [SODAX Builders MCP](/builders-mcp) and it gains live access to the whole SODAX System: supported chains and tokens, real swap quotes, money market rates, intent history, and these docs. It works in Claude, Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf, and any other MCP-capable client.

```json
{
  "mcpServers": {
    "sodax-builders": {
      "url": "https://builders.sodax.com/mcp"
    }
  }
}
```

Use it to scope before you commit: your assistant can read your own codebase, pull real quotes and token lists, and map exactly where SODAX fits in your product. Full tool list and per-client setup at [builders.sodax.com](https://builders.sodax.com/).
