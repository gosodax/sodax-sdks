#### Do I need to write or deploy a contract?

No. SODAX's BNB Chain spoke contracts are already deployed; your integration is TypeScript against `@sodax/sdk` (or the React packages on top of it). You write no on-chain code and hold no admin key for any part of the flow.

#### How does a swap from BNB Chain actually settle?

Your user signs one EVM transaction on BNB Chain that creates an intent. The SODAX relayer carries proof of that transaction to the hub network (Sonic), a solver fills the intent on the destination network, and the output lands at the recipient address there. Swaps into BNB Chain settle as ordinary BNB or BEP-20 balances at the recipient's address. If an intent is never filled, it can be cancelled and the funds recovered.

#### Which assets are supported on BNB Chain?

Two answers, and the difference matters. On BNB Chain itself, <span data-sodax-config="count" data-metric="chain-swap-tokens" data-chain="0x38.bsc">18</span> assets are live for swaps: BNB plus BEP-20 tokens, including stablecoins, BTCB, CAKE, bnUSD, and SODA. <span data-sodax-config="count" data-metric="chain-money-market-tokens" data-chain="0x38.bsc">8</span> assets work in the money market, BNB among them; the two lists overlap but are not identical.

But your users are not limited to the BNB Chain-side list. Every swap is cross-network, so from BNB Chain they can reach all <span data-sodax-config="count" data-metric="swap-tokens">166</span> assets across every SODAX-supported network. Both lists grow as the backend config adds them; read them from the SDK at runtime, or see the live tables in [Networks & Assets](/bnb-chain/networks-and-assets).

#### Why is my USDT amount off by 10^12?

Because USDC and USDT have 18 decimals on BNB Chain and 6 on most other networks. An amount built with 6 decimals is 10^12 too small on BNB Chain; a BNB Chain balance formatted with 6 is 10^12 too large. Build every amount from the `decimals` of the token on the network it lives on, and read `quoted_amount` in the destination token's units. See [Decimals across networks](/bnb-chain/swaps#decimals-across-networks).

#### Why can't I find ETH in the BNB Chain token list?

SODAX lists the Binance-Peg ETH token on BNB Chain as `ETHB`, although the contract's on-chain symbol is `ETH`. Look it up as `ETHB`, or by address.

#### Is there an approve step before swapping or supplying?

For BEP-20 tokens, yes. SODAX's asset manager pulls them through their ERC-20 interface, so `isAllowanceValid` checks the allowance and `approve` sets it, the same as on any EVM network. Native BNB needs no approval.

#### Which RPC endpoint should I use?

Any BNB Smart Chain JSON-RPC endpoint. The SODAX config uses `https://56.rpc.thirdweb.com`; BNB Chain also publishes its own [public endpoints](https://docs.bnbchain.org/bnb-smart-chain/developers/json_rpc/json-rpc-endpoint/), which are rate limited. Pass your choice through `rpcUrl` on `EvmWalletProvider`, per chain in the `@sodax/wallet-sdk-react` config, and in the `Sodax` constructor under `chains[ChainKeys.BSC_MAINNET].rpcUrl` for the SDK's own reads.

#### Is there a testnet?

No. SODAX is mainnet-only on every network, BNB Chain included.

#### What does it cost?

Using SODAX is free: no license, no integration fee, no SDK cost. Trades carry a fixed 0.1% base fee taken by the protocol; on top of that you set your own platform fee and keep it. Your users also pay BNB Chain's network fees in BNB. See [Monetize SDK](/developers/how-to/monetize_sdk).

#### How do I know if something went wrong?

SDK operations return `Result<T>` rather than throwing. Core swap and money market methods return typed error codes (for example `'RELAY_TIMEOUT'`, `'EXECUTION_FAILED'`) you can branch on, with structured context attached.

#### Where are the audits?

Audits for the SODAX protocol are collected on the [Audits](/developers/audits) page.

#### Can my AI coding assistant do the integration?

Largely, yes. Add the [SODAX Builders MCP](/builders-mcp) to Claude, Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf, or any MCP-capable client (`https://builders.sodax.com/mcp`) and it gets live access to the SODAX System: chains, tokens, swap quotes, money market rates, intent history, and auto-synced SDK docs. Use it to scope the integration against your own codebase first, then to write and verify the code. Setup per client is at [builders.sodax.com](https://builders.sodax.com/).
