Add Tron as a spoke chain (`ChainKeys.TRON_MAINNET`, chain type `'TRON'`).

Tron settles through the **MPC relay** rather than the intent relay: a deposit is a transfer to the shared reserve carrying a 32-byte payload-hash memo spliced into the raw transaction, and a withdrawal is authorized with scheme 1 — a TIP-191-wrapped signature the contract recovers the signer from. The reserve address comes from the relay per call rather than being fixed, so a client must pay the address from the current response.

New public surface:

- `@sodax/types` — `TronChainKey`, `TronSpokeChainConfig`, `TronRawTransaction`, `TronRawData`, `TronRawWithdraw`, `TronReturnType`, `TronDepositReturnType`, `TronSendMessageReturnType`, `TronRawTransactionReceipt`, `TronGasEstimate`, `TronUnsignedTransaction` and the `ITronWalletProvider` interface, plus the `tron` entries in `spokeChainConfig` and `supportedTokensByChain` (native TRX and USDT), the `MpcWithdrawMessage` type, and the `MpcRelayChainMap` / `MpcRelayChainKey` registry with `isMpcRelayChainKey` and `getMpcRelayChainInfo`.
- `@sodax/sdk` — `sodax.spoke.tron` (`TronSpokeService`), the MPC relay REST helpers (`getDepositAddress`, `notify`, `waitForDeposit`, `submitWithdraw`, `waitForWithdrawal`), and `SpokeService.settle()`: one settlement seam that swap, bridge and money market call after `create*Intent`, and DEX deposit/withdraw from an MPC-relay chain, routing MPC-relay chains to their relay service and everything else to the intent relay. It reports the hub mint (or the release) as `dstChainTxHash`, never the source id, and an MPC relay that runs out of time fails as `RELAY_TIMEOUT`. A borrow or withdraw from Tron has no Tron tx, so `settle`, money market `borrow`/`withdraw`, DEX `withdraw` and their dapp-kit hooks return `OutboundSettlement<K>`: `{ trackingId, dstChainTxHash }` (`MpcWithdrawalSettlement`) from Tron, the same `TxHashPair` from every other chain. `recovery.withdrawHubAsset` from Tron resolves to that `trackingId` (its analytics report it as `trackingId`, not `txHash`). For a caller that cannot block, `sodax.spoke` also exposes each relay step on its own: `submitMpcDeposit`, `getMpcRelayWithdrawal`, and `submitHubRelease` / `getHubReleaseRecord` / `waitForHubRelease`, keyed by a JSON-safe `HubRelease`. Existing chains keep the same verify-then-relay behavior and the same `SodaxError` codes. `BridgeService.getDetailedStatus` gains a `source: 'mpcRelay'` arm carrying the relay's deposit record.
- `@sodax/sdk` — raw mode on Tron: a deposit returns the checked, unsigned transfer with its memo spliced in and its decoded body (`TronRawTransaction`), and a borrow or withdraw returns a `TronRawWithdraw` to sign and pass to `sodax.spoke.tron.submitSignedWithdraw`. `sodax.spoke.tron.extendExpiration` moves a raw deposit's expiration up to 24 hours ahead before signing. `estimateGas` takes either. A raw `tx` is typed by the operation behind it, so a Tron deposit is a `TronRawTransaction` and a borrow or withdraw a `TronRawWithdraw`, not their union: `DepositReturnType` and `SendMessageReturnType` (equal to `TxReturnType` on every other chain), through `SpokeService` and swap, bridge, money market, DEX and recovery (`IntentTxResult<K, Raw, Op>`). `@sodax/swaps-api` and `@sodax/bridge-api` validate a Tron raw tx strictly and keep its decoded body whole.
- `@sodax/sdk` — `encodeRecipient(spokeChainKey, address)`, the encoding for an address that **receives** funds, as distinct from `encodeAddress`, which encodes an address as an **identity** (deriving a hub wallet, signing a message). They differ only on Tron, where a recipient is the 32-byte word the asset manager expects and an identity is the bare 20-byte account; the chain type picks the encoding, not the relay a chain settles through. Feature services that deliver to a spoke — money market (borrow, withdraw), bridge, recovery, swap intents (`srcAddress`, which a cancel refunds, and `dstAddress`), leverage-yield position origins, partner auto-swap preferences and DEX withdraw — use it. `SetSwapPreferenceParams.outputToken` takes the destination chain's own token format (base58 on Tron).
- `@sodax/types` — `MpcRelayPayoutMinimums` and `getUnmetMpcPayoutMinimum({ token, amount, amountDecimals? })`. The relay's release fee comes out of a payout and nothing is released once it reaches the amount, so the SDK refuses a payout to Tron at or below the minimum with `VALIDATION_FAILED` before signing: bridge and money market borrow (each after its partner fee), money market withdraw, DEX withdraw, recovery, a swap's or leverage-yield withdraw's `minOutputAmount`, and a Tron-source swap's post-fee input, its refund if cancelled. A swap to or from Tron also refuses `allowPartialFill`.
- `@sodax/sdk` — `swaps.waitForIntentDelivery` waits for a solved intent's output on any destination (`IntentDelivery`: the relay packet, or on Tron the release); `getSolvedIntentPacket` stays packet-only and fails for Tron instead of returning a packet the relay never produced.
- `@sodax/wallet-sdk-core` — `TronWalletProvider` in both raw-key and TronLink modes, with `signAndSendTransaction` for a raw `TronRawTransaction`.
- `@sodax/wallet-sdk-react` — `TronXService` and `TronXConnector`, wired into `chainRegistry` and `SodaxWalletConfig`. `useXSignMessage` signs on Tron with TronLink's `signMessageV2`, and refuses once the wallet is on another account than the connected one.

Tron is money-market listed for USDT (TRX's vault is not a money-market reserve), and TRX and USDT are swap-supported in the **staging** solver environment only — read the full staging set with `getStagingSolverTokens`. Bridge and recovery include Tron automatically. Swap and bridge skip the backend submit-tx path for a Tron source, since the backend relays only through the intent relay.

An MPC-relay source chain is refused before signing where the SDK could not confirm its message: leverage yield (vault swaps and positions), concentrated liquidity, staking, and swap `cancelIntent`, which confirms through the intent relay. With no cancel, only a deadline refunds a Tron-source intent, so `createIntent` refuses one without it, and with it a limit order.

Also wires Tron into `GetTxReceiptType`, so `waitForTransactionReceipt` on `tron` resolves to `TronRawTransactionReceipt` instead of `unknown`.
